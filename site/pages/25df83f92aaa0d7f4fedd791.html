Get ready for Season 4 of #ModelMondays, your weekly guide to navigating the evolving world of AI models and agents through the lens of Microsoft Foundry.

Catch up on all the platform updates and model release news in minutes. Then join us for deep-dives and hands-on demos with subject matter experts - from Model Spotlights that explore the capabilities and usage of the featured model, to Tech Spotlights that showcase tools and best practices for model optimization when building AI agents.

Whether you’re deciding what to use or how to get the most out of it, we’ve got you covered! Ask questions live during the session, or join us for Foundry Friday AMAs on Discord to continue the conversation.

Learn more at https://aka.ms/model-mondays - and catch up on episodes from Seasons 1, 2 & 3.
