We have a series of insightful Data Factory in Microsoft Fabric sessions for you to learn more about data integration in Fabric. From broad sessions on the roadmap for Data Factory and Database Mirroring to deep dive sessions on Dataflows Gen2 and upgrade pathways from Azure Data Factory to Fabric Data Factory, we'll cover everything you need to know about Data Fabric in Microsoft Fabric.

    Simplifying Workflow Orchestration with Apache Airflow in Microsoft Fabric

    7:00 PM - 8:00 PM (UTC)

    Join us for an in-depth session on leveraging Apache Airflow within Microsoft Fabric to streamline your complex workflow orchestration needs. Discover how Microsoft Fabric’s managed Apache Airflow environments allow you to focus on business logic while Microsoft handles the creation, scaling, and management of clusters. We’ll explore the benefits of using Apache Airflow DAGs (Directed Acyclic Graphs) for efficient data workflows, and demonstrate how this integration simplifies the development, scheduling, and monitoring of your data pipelines. Whether you’re new to Apache Airflow or looking to optimize your existing workflows, this session will provide valuable insights and practical tips to enhance your data orchestration capabilities.
