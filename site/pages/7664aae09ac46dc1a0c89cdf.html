Topic: Agents

Join us for an inspiring journey through AI technology and its practical applications, featuring content from the worldwide Microsoft AI Tour and led by industry-leading MVPs and Global AI Community leaders. From deep dives into machine learning and AI architectures to deploying intelligent applications, these expert-driven sessions cover a wide range of the hottest AI topics and will help you start building your own practical AI solutions today.
