Azure Developers - .NET Day 2024

April 30, 2024 | 3:00 - 8:00 PM (UTC) Coordinated Universal Time

  • Format:
  • Livestream

Topic: Coding, Languages, and Frameworks

Language: English

Azure Developers - .NET Day is back for its second installment enabling .NET developers to build for the cloud, stay ahead of the curb, and maximize developer productivity. Join us to discover the latest and greatest features of Azure designed for .NET developers and their applications. You'll learn cutting-edge cloud development techniques that can save you time and money, while providing your customers with the best experience possible. During the event, you'll hear directly from the experts behind the most sought-after cloud services for developers, spanning AI, app development/compute, data services, serverless computing, cloud-native computing, and developer productivity. Don't miss this chance to participate and engage with the team throughout the day. Join us and take your cloud development skills to the next level!
