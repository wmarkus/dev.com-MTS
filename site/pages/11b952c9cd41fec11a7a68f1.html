Language: English

  • Events in this Series:

Set yourself up for success with this in-depth PL-300 certification exam preparation series. Designed for Power BI Data Analysts ready to demonstrate their expertise, this five-session series covers all four key exam sections: preparing data, modeling data, visualizing & analyzing data, and managing & securing data. We’ll end the series with a final session focused on preparing for exam day.

Each session dives into the essential concepts, tools, and techniques you'll need to pass the exam. Whether you're new to Power BI or building on your existing skills, this series gives you the knowledge and confidence to take the PL-300 exam and to take your data analyst skills to the next level.

Live sessions will be available in English in two time zones. If you are looking for a later session, visit this series.
