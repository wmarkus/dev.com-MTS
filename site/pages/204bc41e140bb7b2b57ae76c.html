Let's learn about the Model Context Protocol (MCP), a cutting-edge framework designed to standardize interactions between AI models and client applications. Through this beginner-friendly workshop, we'll introduce you to MCP and guide you through creating your first MCP server.

We have workshops in a range of options for global developers. Select the coding language and spoken language workshop of your choice below!
