Agentic DevOps Live is your central hub for learning, implementing, and mastering the tools and services that bring AI-powered automation into the software lifecycle. Each session offers practical insights, demos, and actionable playbooks to help you apply Agentic DevOps effectively.

Join live or access on demand—return anytime for blueprints, adoption guides, and hands-on resources designed to boost productivity, accelerate delivery, and enable innovation with confidence and quality.
