Agents are only as good as the context they can reason over. Microsoft IQ is the intelligence layer that gives AI agents that context, spanning how your people work (Work IQ), how your business is modeled in data (Fabric IQ), your institutional knowledge and authoritative content (Foundry IQ), and fresh signal from the public web (Web IQ). Microsoft IQ Live is a bi-weekly series for developers building enterprise agents with the Microsoft IQ stack.

Across the series we explore the full stack through hands-on, demo-led sessions, grounding every concept in working code and real architectures you can adapt to your own agents. Tune in every other Thursday at 9 AM PT on Reactor.
