Topic: Personal and Business Development

Language: English, Hebrew

  • Events in this Series:

This series is dedicated to empowering women entrepreneurs on their journey to secure investment. We delve into the strategies, challenges, and successes experienced by women founders as they navigate the investment landscape. Each session offers insights, practical advice, and inspirational stories to guide and motivate women in building financially robust businesses. Join us as we explore the keys to unlocking investment opportunities and fostering growth and innovation in women-led ventures.
