Make Azure AI Real

  • Format:
  • Livestream

Topic: Using AI Products, Core AI, Agents

Language: English

  • Events in this Series:
  • 15

Microsoft Reactor’s Make Azure AI Real series is a collection of AI livestreams that are focused on AI technical content from experts at Microsoft. You will learn how to use Microsoft AI tools and services, such as Azure Cognitive Services, Azure OpenAI Service, and more. You will also get insights into the latest AI trends and best practices from Microsoft AI experts. Join us for this exciting series and discover how to make AI real for your projects and goals.
