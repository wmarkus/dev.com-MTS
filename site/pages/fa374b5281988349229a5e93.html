Level up your data and analytics skills at Fabric Data Days, an interactive event series designed for data professionals, career changers, and students alike.

Join expert-led sessions on certifications, data engineering, analytics, data visualization, and modern Fabric workflows. Explore practical tips, real-world examples, and interactive challenges that help you build your skills, showcase your work, and connect with the global Fabric community.

Whether you’re aiming to get certified, transition into a new role, or push your creativity in data visualization, Fabric Data Days gives you the tools and inspiration to take your skills further.
