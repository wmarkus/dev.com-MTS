Topic: Core AI, Agents, Personal and Business Development, AI Applications, Infrastructure for AI

Language: English

Explore and innovate with AI and the Microsoft Cloud through the Microsoft Student Innovator Series! Review our sessions below and click "details" to learn more and register. We'll be adding new sessions through the end of the year, so don't forget to check back frequently and register for events that interest you!

This exciting program offers a range of events designed to help you take your next steps as tech innovator. Events will focus on how to be successful in the Imagine Cup, Microsoft's premier global technology startup competition for student founders, and deep dive sessions on Azure AI and Microsoft for Startups Founders Hub to help you start building on the Microsoft Cloud.

    Azure OpenAI and Azure AI Search (Python) with Samuel Bamgbola

    5:00 PM - 6:00 PM (UTC)

    This session is led by a Gold Milestone Microsoft Learn Student Ambassador. Explore the power of Azure OpenAI and Azure AI Search with a Python-based ChatGPT-like app. This session will guide you through creating a ChatGPT-like experience using your data, leveraging the Retrieval Augmented Generation pattern. Access GPT models via Azure OpenAI Service and utilize Azure AI Search for efficient data indexing and retrieval. The session includes hands-on examples with sample data, featuring Contoso Electronics—a fictitious company—where employees can inquire about company benefits, policies, and job specifics.
