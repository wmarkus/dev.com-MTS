Are you a startup founder or a developer looking for ways to leverage the power of Generative AI to ideate, develop an innovative solution, better your business workflow or even scale?

Join us for a series of exciting and informative sessions where we will introduce you to the basics of Generative AI and show you how you can use AI to enhance your creativity, productivity, and efficiency. Walk through demos and workshops to experience it yourself.
You will learn how the latest AI trends and tools, use Microsoft Co-pilot to help you ideate, develop, grow and scale your solution, and also use Microsoft tools and GitHub to low code.

Finally, hear from startups and developers who have benefitted from AI tools like Microsoft Co-Pilot, GitHub, Microsoft OpenAI etc. to create innovative solutions and scale their businesses.

PS: We got this blurb out in a few seconds with Microsoft Co-pilot (and this is just the tip of the iceberg to what Microsoft Co-pilot offers). We can’t wait to show you the potential of Generative AI and how it can help you grow your business.

Register now and get ready to experience the power of Generative AI!
