🚀 Microsoft DevConnect

💫 Overview
Technology is advancing at breakneck speed, and those who stay ahead of the curve will shape the future. The low-code development market is surging, expected to reach $187 billion by 2030, proving that businesses are rapidly embracing faster, more efficient ways to build solutions. Meanwhile, Microsoft’s AI business now generates over $13 billion annually, with AI-powered tools like Copilot revolutionizing how we work and create. These aren’t just trends—they’re massive shifts redefining industries and opening new opportunities for those ready to embrace them.

Microsoft DevConnect—hosted by Microsoft Learn Student Ambassadors Nigeria and Microsoft Reactors—brings together students, developers, and professionals to explore the power of Cloud Computing, Artificial Intelligence (AI) & Copilot, Low-Code Development (Power Platform), .NET, and Software Engineering. This hybrid event is designed to equip you with practical skills and insights needed to build real-world solutions, boost productivity, and stay competitive.
