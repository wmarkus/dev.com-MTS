The AI-102 series offers an in-depth exploration of designing and implementing AI solutions on Microsoft Azure. Throughout this comprehensive series, you will delve into crucial aspects of AI development, gaining the knowledge and skills required to excel in creating intelligent solutions. Across six informative sessions, we will cover key topics to provide you with a robust understanding of Microsoft Azure's AI capabilities, guiding you through the process of designing, implementing, and optimizing AI solutions. Topics covered include AI solution architecture, data storage and processing, natural language processing, computer vision, speech services, and more. By the end of this series, you will be well-equipped to tackle the AI-102 exam and apply your expertise in real-world scenarios, contributing to the success of your organization's AI initiatives. Join us on this educational journey to unlock the full potential of Microsoft Azure in the realm of artificial intelligence.

Defining and configuring a knowledge store. Persisting enrichment outputs (e.g., key phrases, entities, image content) in a queryable form. Accessing and analyzing the stored data using tools like Power BI or custom dashboards. Hands-on Exercise: Build and test an enrichment pipeline with a connected knowledge store. Techniques to extract, project, and store enriched data. Real-world use cases for building intelligent knowledge apps.
